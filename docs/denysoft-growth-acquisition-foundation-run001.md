## Commercial objective

Build a repeatable, measurable acquisition system around the live Denysoft experience:

`source -> visit -> evidence -> discuss -> prepare email -> human qualification -> scoped opportunity`

The site remains the evidence and trust layer. Acquisition should bring the right people into that layer rather than turning Denysoft into a high-volume generic lead-generation agency.

## Positioning decision

Denysoft should **not** lead with generic categories such as:

- AI automation agency
- web design agency
- chatbot agency
- software outsourcing shop
- full-service digital agency

Those categories are crowded and reduce the value of Denysoft's current evidence model.

The commercial framing is:

> Denysoft helps owner-led and growth-stage organizations build digital products, improve operating systems and put AI to useful work — with visible human judgment and evidence.

For partner channels:

> Denysoft adds product, frontend, systems and governed AI capability behind an existing client relationship, with clear boundaries and portable handoffs.

## Ideal Customer Profiles

**Typical shape**

- approximately 10–150 people, or a smaller team with meaningful transaction volume
- one or more locations, service teams, appointment flows, case/work-order flows, or customer handoffs
- customer experience depends on coordination across people, systems, messages and evidence

**Useful signals**

- multi-location growth

- significant WhatsApp / phone / email coordination

## Commercial offer architecture

These are internal offer shapes, not public pricing commitments.

### Offer A — Challenge Framing / System Diagnostic

**Purpose:** low-risk entry when the problem is still ambiguous.

**Output:** problem framing, workflow/system map, constraints, opportunity options, recommended next step, evidence/unknowns.

**Good for:** all ICPs.

### Offer B — Product / Experience Build

**Purpose:** design and implement a customer-facing digital product or service experience.

**Output:** specification, UX, visual system where needed, implementation, QA, handoff/deployment evidence.

**Good for:** ICP-2 and ICP-3.

### Offer C — Operations System Modernization

**Purpose:** improve how requests, approvals, handoffs, records and status move through a business.

**Output:** workflow model, system architecture, integrations, operator experience, controlled automation.

**Good for:** ICP-1 and ICP-2.

### Offer D — Governed AI Capability Pilot

**Purpose:** test whether AI creates useful leverage inside an existing product or workflow.

**Output:** bounded use case, provider/tool decision, eval criteria, safety/authority boundaries, pilot implementation and evidence.

**Good for:** ICP-1, ICP-2 and ICP-4.

### Offer E — Partner Capability Track

**Purpose:** add specialist product/technical/AI capacity to an agency or consultancy without taking over its client relationship.

**Output:** scoped capability, agreed boundary, implementation artifacts, evidence and portable handoff.

**Good for:** ICP-4.

## Qualification model

Research accounts are scored internally from 0–10. This is a prioritization aid, not a claim about the company.

- **Observed operating complexity (0–2):** public evidence of locations, teams, service lines, workflows or scale.
- **Evidence bridge (0–2):** how naturally a Denysoft case/evidence family supports the conversation.
- **Potential system leverage (0–2):** plausible opportunity for product/workflow/client-system improvement.
- **Reachability (0–2):** clear company-level contact path, founder-led identity, or partner inquiry path.
- **Geographic / collaboration fit (0–1):** Canada, US, El Salvador or cross-border relevance.
- **Non-commodity fit (0–1):** opportunity appears broader than a simple brochure website request.

### Qualification states

- `RESEARCHED` — public account evidence captured.
- `VALIDATE` — promising hypothesis; needs manual review before any contact.
- `HOLD` — useful account but weak immediate evidence bridge.
- `DO_NOT_CONTACT` — conflict, poor fit, unclear legitimacy, or other concern.
- `APPROVED_FOR_OUTREACH` — requires a later Human gate; no account has this state in Run 001.

## Channel architecture

### 1. Founder-led LinkedIn

Role: credibility + conversation surface.

Content should show:

- product/system decisions
- before/after thinking, not vanity visuals
- evidence from active work without overstating outcomes
- useful AI with boundaries
- small-business operating problems explained clearly

Primary CTA: relevant Denysoft evidence page or `/discuss` with UTM.

### 2. Account-based outreach

Role: deliberate one-to-one acquisition.

Use only after Human approval.

Sequence concept:

`observed signal -> relevant hypothesis -> evidence bridge -> low-friction conversation`

Do not open with generic services, AI hype or a mass template.

### 3. Partner channel

Role: agency / consultancy referrals and white-label delivery.

Target firms that already own strategy, creative, marketing or client relationships but occasionally need deeper product/frontend/systems/AI implementation.

### 4. Search / SEO

Role: compounding discovery.

Near-term content themes:

- digital product partner for service businesses
- customer portal / client portal for professional services
- service operations workflow modernization
- AI-enabled business systems with human oversight
- white-label product / frontend / AI delivery partner

Do not publish low-value keyword pages merely to increase page count.

### 5. Referral / ecosystem

Role: warm access through chambers, BIAs, business networks, professional communities and existing relationships.

Memberships or paid sponsorships are not authorized in this run. Public directories and free events may be evaluated separately.

## 30-day launch operating rhythm

### Week 1 — Foundation + account research

- finalize ICPs and internal offer map
- build first account research set
- prepare UTM links
- prepare founder-led content topics
- prepare outreach drafts, but send nothing
- establish weekly measurement sheet/template

### Week 2 — Evidence-led organic launch

After separate Human approval to publish:

- publish 2–3 founder-led LinkedIn posts
- route each post to one relevant evidence page, not always Home
- observe traffic and CTA behavior
- refine account list based on actual engagement and fit

### Week 3 — Controlled one-to-one outreach

Only after separate Human approval:

- start with a small batch of highly qualified accounts
- no bulk automation
- personalize to one observed signal
- track response and conversation quality, not just send volume

### Week 4 — Learn + decide

- compare channel quality
- identify which offer/account pair creates actual conversations
- stop weak channels
- expand only where evidence supports it
- decide whether a CRM import, Search Console connection, paid experiment or additional content deserves a separate run

## Message principles

Every message should satisfy this sequence:

1. **Observation** — a specific public signal about the business.
2. **Hypothesis** — a possible opportunity, stated as something to validate, not a diagnosis.
3. **Relevance** — one Denysoft evidence bridge.
4. **Low-friction CTA** — short conversation, not an immediate proposal.

Avoid:

- “I noticed your website is bad.”
- “We can 10x your business.”
- fake familiarity
- fake urgency
- AI hype
- invented metrics
- pretending a hypothesis is a confirmed internal problem

## Competitive pattern observed during Run 001 research

Public 2026 market research shows many boutique studios now lead with combinations of web/app development, AI automation, white-label capacity, performance or founder-led service. Examples reviewed include Novi Toronto, Meevo Digital, Rapid Fire, Syntara, DigiCoders, AgencyRelay, Partnerly, PlinkSolutions, Caverna Tecnológica and b-viral.

Implication: Denysoft should differentiate on **judgment + systems + governed AI + evidence**, not on claiming to be another boutique AI/web agency.
