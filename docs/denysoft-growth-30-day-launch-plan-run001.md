# Denysoft Growth — 30-Day Launch Plan — Run 001

## Status

Planning artifact only. No publishing, outreach sends, CRM imports, paid ads, purchases or Production changes are authorized by this document.

## Operating principle

Denysoft should earn attention by showing how it thinks, then route interested people to evidence that matches their problem.

The working loop is:

`research -> evidence-led content -> observed engagement -> qualified conversation -> learning`

Not:

`mass outreach -> generic pitch -> forced demo`

## Week 1 — Prepare the machine

### Day 1

- Confirm ICP-1 through ICP-4.
- Review the researched account list for obvious conflicts, weak evidence or inaccurate assumptions.
- Select a small internal shortlist for each ICP; do not contact.

### Day 2

- Build UTM links for:
  - Home
  - Solutions
  - Work
  - Taller Express
  - Mastertax
  - Baltica Salon
  - Partners
  - Discuss
- Use only the canonical UTM contract in the growth foundation.

### Day 3

Prepare first four founder-led content briefs:

1. **A service business is a system, not just a website.**
   - Evidence: Taller Express.
   - Audience: automotive, logistics, field/service operations.

2. **AI should enter after the workflow is understood.**
   - Evidence: AXOM.
   - Audience: owners evaluating AI/automation.

3. **A premium digital experience still needs operational clarity.**
   - Evidence: Baltica / Denysoft visual authority.
   - Audience: boutique/premium service businesses.

4. **Professional services can modernize intake without automating judgment away.**
   - Evidence: Mastertax + AXOM.
   - Audience: accounting, legal, advisory.

### Day 4

Prepare account-specific research notes for the first controlled outreach batch. Each note must contain:

- one verifiable public observation
- one hypothesis to validate
- one Denysoft evidence bridge
- one short CTA
- no invented pain

### Day 5

Create the first weekly baseline record:

- visitor/session baseline
- top landing pages
- Work / Solutions / Discuss traffic
- `commercial_cta_click`
- `commercial_email_prepare`
- real inbound messages
- qualified conversations

If traffic is too low to interpret, record that fact instead of manufacturing a conclusion.

## Week 2 — Organic evidence launch

Requires separate Human approval before publishing from personal/company accounts.

### Publishing cadence

- 2 strong LinkedIn posts minimum; 3 maximum.
- 1 evidence-led short-form post may be reused bilingually if it remains natural rather than mechanically translated.
- No daily posting requirement.

### Post structure

- concrete problem pattern
- useful point of view
- one evidence example
- link only when it genuinely helps
- no engagement bait

### Routing rule

Do not route every post to Home.

Examples:

- service operations post -> Taller Express
- AI governance post -> AXOM
- agency collaboration post -> Partners
- professional services post -> Mastertax or Solutions

### End-of-week review

Look for:

- which topic caused profile/site visits
- which evidence pages received traffic
- whether Discuss traffic rose
- whether CTA events appeared
- any real inbound message

No decision should be made from vanity engagement alone.

## Week 3 — Controlled one-to-one acquisition

Requires a separate Human outreach gate.

### Batch size

Start with 5–8 accounts only.

Recommended first-batch composition after manual review:

- 2 operational service businesses
- 2 professional-services firms
- 1 premium/boutique service business
- 1–2 partner-channel accounts

### Outreach rules

- one person/company at a time
- no bulk automation
- no purchased list
- no “spray and pray”
- no fabricated personalization
- no claim that Denysoft has audited internal systems
- do not attach proposals in the first contact

### What qualifies a reply

A useful reply indicates at least one of:

- acknowledged problem or opportunity
- curiosity about a Denysoft evidence example
- willingness to discuss workflow/system context
- referral to a more appropriate person

A polite “not interested” is a valid signal and should not trigger repeated follow-up.

## Week 4 — Evidence review and channel decision

### Review dimensions

For each active channel:

- traffic quality
- evidence-page depth
- CTA behavior
- actual inbound conversations
- qualification quality
- time cost
- repeatability

### Possible decisions

- continue and deepen
- narrow to one ICP
- change evidence routing
- pause a weak channel
- prepare a dedicated offer page
- prepare CRM import
- prepare direct Google Search Console connection
- prepare a bounded paid experiment

Each downstream action becomes a separate governed run if it mutates external systems, spends money, changes Production or sends outreach at scale.

## Initial 30-day success definition

Success is **not** a promised lead count.

Run 001 launch success means:

- a stable acquisition operating rhythm exists
- real source/traffic data is being captured
- at least one ICP/offer pair produces observable engagement or conversation evidence
- weak assumptions are retired quickly
- next investment decisions are based on evidence rather than enthusiasm

## Stop rules

Pause the channel or message if:

- replies show repeated mismatch between the offer and account problem
- evidence cannot support the claim being made
- acquisition depends on buying tools before proving the motion
- outreach requires pretending to know private internal problems
- channel effort materially exceeds the quality of resulting conversations
