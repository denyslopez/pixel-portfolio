# Denysoft Evidence-Led Content Briefs — Run 001

## Status

Draft briefs only. Publishing requires a separate Human gate.

Each brief should become a concise founder-led post, not a corporate press release. The post may point to Denysoft evidence when that improves understanding.

## Brief 01 — A service business is a system, not just a website

**Audience:** automotive, logistics, field service, multi-location service operations.

**Core point:** The customer sees a service. The business operates a chain of intake, diagnosis, approval, handoffs, status and evidence. Improving only the front-end page often leaves the real system untouched.

**Evidence bridge:** Taller Express.

**Useful example frame:**

`Request -> operator decision -> workflow -> status -> evidence`

**CTA:** invite the reader to inspect the Taller Express evidence or discuss a service workflow.

**Recommended destination:** `/en/work/taller-express` or `/es/work/taller-express`.

## Brief 02 — AI should enter after the workflow is understood

**Audience:** owners considering chatbots, agents, AI automation or copilots.

**Core point:** Before adding intelligence, identify the workflow, authority boundaries, failure cost and evidence needed to know whether the system improved.

**Evidence bridge:** AXOM.

**Useful frame:**

`Signal -> System -> Intelligence -> Evidence`

**Avoid:** arguing that every company needs agents.

**Recommended destination:** `/en/axom` or `/es/axom`.

## Brief 03 — Premium digital experience still needs operational clarity

**Audience:** salons, wellness, boutique real estate, premium local services.

**Core point:** Brand and interface quality matter, but the experience breaks when booking, follow-up, context and service handoffs do not match the promise made by the design.

**Evidence bridge:** Baltica Salon + Denysoft visual authority.

**Useful frame:**

`Promise -> interaction -> service -> follow-up`

**Recommended destination:** Baltica selected work.

## Brief 04 — Professional services can modernize intake without automating judgment away

**Audience:** accountants, lawyers, advisors, consultancies.

**Core point:** There is a large space between doing everything manually and delegating judgment to AI. Intake, routing, document collection, status and preparation can improve while professional judgment remains human.

**Evidence bridge:** Mastertax + AXOM.

**Recommended destination:** Mastertax selected work or `/solutions`.

## Brief 05 — What a good AI pilot should prove

**Audience:** founders and operators evaluating AI spend.

**Core point:** A useful pilot should answer a bounded question and produce evidence, not merely a demo.

**Checklist:**

- what task is being improved?
- what is the baseline?
- what failure modes matter?
- where does a human approve?
- what would make the pilot worth continuing?

**Evidence bridge:** AXOM governance language.

**Recommended destination:** `/axom`.

## Brief 06 — Agency capacity without agency theater

**Audience:** agencies, consultancies, creative studios.

**Core point:** A partner should be able to add product/technical/AI depth without competing for the relationship, hiding ownership or creating an unportable black box.

**Evidence bridge:** Denysoft Partners.

**Useful frame:**

`Partner relationship -> defined capability gap -> Denysoft delivery -> portable handoff`

**Recommended destination:** `/partners`.

## Writing standard

Every post should contain:

- one concrete operating insight
- one useful distinction
- one evidence bridge when relevant
- one optional CTA

Do not include:

- fabricated client metrics
- “10x” language
- generic AI-news reposting without a Denysoft point of view
- daily-posting filler
- claims of scale Denysoft cannot evidence

## Bilingual rule

English is primary for Canada/US reach. Spanish versions should be editorial adaptations, not literal translations. If a Spanish version becomes materially longer, preserve hierarchy and clarity rather than sentence-for-sentence symmetry.
