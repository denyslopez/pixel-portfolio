## Purpose

Protect the distinction between Denys Lopez, Denysoft and AXOM while allowing the Denysoft platform to demonstrate both proven work and forward momentum.

## Brand roles

### Denys Lopez
Personal professional authority and founder identity. Represents multidisciplinary experience across design, engineering, product, growth and AI.

### Denysoft
Founder-led digital studio / commercial service brand. Owns conventional and advanced digital client work that is correctly attributable to Denys/Denysoft.

### AXOM
Separate AI-native product/software ecosystem and governed factory. AXOM products are not to be relabeled as Denysoft client work.

## Home content architecture

### 1. HERO
Position Denysoft / Denys as the commercial entry point.

Canonical geographic narrative:

ENG: `Based in Canada. Operating from El Salvador and working across the United States and Latin America.`

ESP: `Basado en Canadá. Operando desde El Salvador y trabajando con mercados en Estados Unidos y Latinoamérica.`

### 2. SELECTED WORK
Only work correctly attributable to Denys/Denysoft.

Use mature/public-safe evidence. Current preferred flagship candidates include:
- Reveal Studio
- Taller Express, only to the extent the displayed scope is attributable and public-safe
- Villas de San Luis or another mature commercial project

The section proves execution history and commercial craft.

### 3. SELECTED CLIENT WORK
Compact archive of attributable client/studio work. No inflation into full case studies where evidence is thin.

### 4. BUILDING AXOM
AXOM itself is the strategic achievement.

Suggested positioning:

`Building AXOM — an AI-native product and software factory designed to turn real business problems into governed digital products.`

This section may explain capabilities such as governed agentic execution, product engineering, human approval, provider/model flexibility and evidence-driven delivery where public-safe.

### 5. AXOM IN PRODUCTION / PRODUCTS BUILT THROUGH AXOM
Products such as TLBN, AI Academy / GenAI Bootcamp, AXOM Client Hub or later AXOM products may appear only as evidence of AXOM's production capability.

They must NOT be presented as Denysoft client projects.

Each item must expose an explicit state when shown, e.g.:
- IN DEVELOPMENT
- PRIVATE PREVIEW
- PILOT
- VALIDATION
- R&D

Principle:

`Do not convert work in progress into proof of results. Convert it into proof of momentum.`

Only public-safe products may be shown.

### 6. LAB / WHAT'S NEXT
Experiments and explorations attributable to Denys/Denysoft, such as:
- Diaspora Heat Map
- creative AI interfaces
- generative interaction experiments
- public-safe prototypes
- motion / GPU studies

This section proves curiosity and forward-looking capability without claiming completed commercial outcomes.

### 7. CAPABILITIES / SERVICES
Commercially clear offer surface. Must remain understandable to a non-technical buyer.

### 8. ABOUT
Narrative progression: visual design → web/frontend → UX/product → growth → product engineering → AI-native systems.

### 9. CONTACT
Direct conversion to email and temporary WhatsApp handoff. AI Website Concierge remains deferred.

## Attribution rules

1. Never attribute AXOM-produced products to Denysoft merely because the same founder is involved.
2. Denysoft may state that its founder is building AXOM.
3. AXOM products may be referenced as evidence of the AXOM system, with their state explicitly disclosed.
4. Never present in-progress work as completed results.
5. Never fabricate metrics, outcomes or maturity.
6. Generated / licensed conceptual visuals may illustrate ideas, but must not masquerade as delivered product evidence.
7. Client confidentiality and public-safe evidence override marketing value.

## Narrative ladder

The homepage should communicate three different proof layers:

`PAST WORK PROVES EXPERIENCE`

`CURRENT WORK PROVES MOMENTUM`

`AXOM PROVES WHERE WE ARE GOING`

This ladder is approved as a content/brand principle, not necessarily as literal on-page copy.

## Key-screen implication

The next R3 design board must visually distinguish:

- Selected Work — proven / attributable
- Building AXOM — strategic system / future-facing
- AXOM in Production — status-driven evidence, not portfolio trophies
- Lab — experimentation

The visual language can be shared, but labeling, hierarchy and status treatment must prevent brand attribution ambiguity.
