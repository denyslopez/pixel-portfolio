# Denysoft External Communications — Human Confirmation Control

## Status

**BINDING OPERATING CONTROL — effective 2026-09-16**

This control was established after Human direction that no external communication may be sent on Denysoft's behalf without an explicit, immediate confirmation for the actual communication.

## Core rule

Research, preparation, qualification, drafting, batch approval, campaign planning, channel selection, or a prior general authorization **never by themselves authorize transmission**.

Before any real external communication is sent or submitted, the Human must be shown, in the current interaction:

1. **Recipient** — the exact person, company, or destination.
2. **Channel** — email, web form, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, social DM, or other external channel.
3. **Sending identity** — the exact account/name/domain that the recipient will see when known.
4. **Exact content** — the final subject and body/message that will be transmitted.
5. **Real-world consequence** — an explicit statement that approval will cause a real third party to receive the communication.

The Human must then provide a clear confirmation to **send/submit now**. Without that immediate confirmation, the system may only prepare or save a draft.

## Applies to

This control applies to, at minimum:

- outbound email
- contact-form submissions
- partner/vendor applications
- LinkedIn messages or connection notes
- WhatsApp/SMS
- social direct messages
- public comments/posts/replies directed at an external party
- CRM actions that trigger an external communication
- invitations, meeting requests, or calendar invitations sent to third parties
- follow-ups, corrections, apologies, or clarification messages

## Non-authorizing phrases

The following are **not sufficient by themselves** to transmit a real communication:

- "prepare"
- "proceed"
- "continue"
- approval of a strategy, campaign, batch, run, draft, or PR
- approval that occurred before the final recipient/channel/from-identity/content were displayed

## Required execution gate

A valid transmission gate should be explicit, for example:

`SEND NOW — <recipient> — <channel> — exact message shown above`

For a small batch, one approval may cover multiple messages only if **every recipient, channel, sending identity, subject, and exact body has just been displayed together** and the Human explicitly authorizes the whole named batch to be sent now.

## Channel substitution rule

If the approved channel is unavailable, blocked, requires login, requires payment/credits, or otherwise cannot be used exactly as approved:

- stop that recipient
- do not silently switch channels
- do not infer an alternate address or account
- prepare an alternate public business channel only
- show the alternate route and exact message
- obtain a new immediate send confirmation

## Identity and authorship rule

AI may research and draft on Denysoft's behalf, but it must not create the misleading impression that the Human personally authored a message without the Human seeing the exact final text before transmission.

External messages must contain only supportable statements about Denysoft, its projects, its capabilities, and the prospect's publicly observable facts. Prospect pain, internal problems, ROI, savings, revenue, adoption, or operational conditions remain hypotheses until validated.

## Follow-up rule

No reply, follow-up, clarification, correction, or second attempt is automatic. Every subsequent outbound communication requires the same immediate Human confirmation gate.

## Existing Batch 01 state

The already-completed Batch 01 external actions remain historical evidence. This control governs all further communication, including any follow-up to those recipients and any attempt to contact the unsent accounts.

## Default behavior

When in doubt: **DRAFT, DO NOT SEND.**
