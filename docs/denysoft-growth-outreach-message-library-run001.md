## Core rule

The first message is not a proposal. It is a short, relevant hypothesis with an evidence bridge.

Structure:

`observation -> hypothesis -> relevance -> low-friction CTA`

## Forbidden patterns

Do not use:

- “I audited your business.”
- “Your current system is losing you money.”
- “We guarantee X% growth.”
- “AI will replace your staff.”
- fake compliments
- fake referrals
- false deadlines
- repeated follow-ups after a clear no
- automated personalization that has not been manually verified
